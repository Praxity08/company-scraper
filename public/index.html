How it works

From a website to a reviewed draft email, one careful step at a time

You type up to 5 company websites. For each one, the tool reads the company's own pages, writes down only facts it can quote word for word, decides whether the company is worth contacting, finds one person, picks what to talk about, and drafts an email. The draft only ever goes to your own inbox. Nothing is ever sent to the company.

Each step needs one click, in order. Nothing runs by itself.

Step 0

Scrape

Reads the company's own website and keeps the text of up to 7 pages: homepage, pricing, about, careers, security, customers and press.

  • Polite: a 1–2 second pause between requests, and the site's robots.txt rules are respected.
  • LinkedIn is never visited. Companies on the do-not-contact list are skipped and nothing is fetched.
  • A missing page says why: no such page (404), blocked, redirected to the homepage, or JavaScript-only.

About 20 seconds per website · free

Step 1

Company picture

The AI (Gemini) reads the saved pages and lists facts: what they do, funding, team size, certifications, open jobs, enterprise plans, named customers, countries.

  • Every fact must come with the page link and an exact quote. Code checks the quote word for word against the page.
  • Anything that doesn't match is thrown away and counted as "rejected". Missing means unknown — nothing is guessed.

About 30 seconds · 1 of 50 AI steps a day

Step 2

Checks & decision

Fixed rules (the Evidence Map) answer four questions from the quoted facts. If a key detail is unknown, it may run up to 2 web searches: one for the funding round and one for "[company] data breach".

  • Go: carry on. Flag: you must read the evidence and click approve. No-Go: stop.
  • A search result only counts if its quote is word for word in the result.

Up to ~2 minutes · up to 2 searches

Step 3

Contact

Hunter looks up people at the company's domain and the tool picks one, in this order: security / CISO / compliance / GRC, then CTO / head of engineering, then a founder.

  • Only after a Go, or a Flag you approved. A No-Go never gets a lookup (except on a TEST ONLY run).
  • One lookup per company, ever. Confidence under 80 shows LOW CONFIDENCE. Contacts are deleted after 30 days.

A few seconds · 1 of 2 Hunter lookups a day (shared)

Step 4

Pain points

Picks up to 2 typical challenges for the contact's role from your list (docs/pain-points.md): security people, engineering leaders or founders.

  • They are typical for the role, never claims about the company.
  • Some only count when the company's own site shows a matching fact (an open compliance job, a team size, or a certification in progress).

Instant · no AI · free

Step 5

Email draft

The AI only suggests opening lines ("hooks"). Code checks each one, and your fixed wording (docs/email-settings.md) writes the rest.

  • A hook must be word for word from the company's own pages and 4–30 words long.
  • Banned: funding words, negative news, menu or button text, image text. No hook means no email.
  • The draft aims for 80–100 words.

About 30 seconds · 1 of 50 AI steps a day

Step 6

Send to my inbox

Sends the draft as plain text to your own inbox only, with a review header at the top saying who it is meant for and where the hook came from.

  • You must type the exact command, for example send fake-co.com, hook 1.
  • Never twice. If the email service refuses it, nothing was sent and you can try again. If the answer is unclear, it locks so you can check your inbox first.

A few seconds · 1 of 3 inbox sends a day (shared)

Afterwards

What you do

Read the draft in your inbox. If you want to use it, you send it yourself from your own email account, after your own checks.

  • Every company keeps a record here: facts, checks, decision, contact, pain points, draft, status and next steps.
  • Reopen any company from Recent runs.

Safety rules

  • Never emails a company. The only email goes to your own inbox.
  • Every fact, answer and hook shows its exact quote and a link to where it came from.
  • Negative news can qualify a company but is never used in an email.
  • Do-not-contact companies are always skipped.
  • No logins: everyone with the link sees the same runs, so keep the link private.

TEST ONLY runs

  • Chosen before you scrape and locked to those companies.
  • Labelled everywhere, and "[TEST ONLY]" goes in front of the email subject.
  • Lets Hunter run even on a No-Go, so you can try the whole journey.
  • While your pain-point list and email wording are still DRAFT, they only work on TEST ONLY runs.

Daily limits (India time, shared)

  • Hunter lookups: 2 a day.
  • AI steps (company pictures + drafts): 50 a day.
  • Sends to your inbox: 3 a day.
  • They reset at midnight IST. The sidebar shows what's left.

Questions

Why is a company No-Go when it looks like a good fit?

Usually because its website doesn't say enough. If two or more checks are unknown, it's No-Go: missing means unknown, and nothing is guessed. Open step 2 to see which checks were unknown.

Why did it say "No hook found"?

Every suggested opening line failed a rule: not word for word, too short or long, about funding, negative news, or menu text. The rejected lines and reasons are listed in step 5. No hook means no email, on purpose.

Can I approve a No-Go?

No. Only a Flag can be approved. A TEST ONLY run lets Hunter run on a No-Go so you can test the rest of the journey, but those emails are labelled TEST ONLY.

Why do I have to type the send command?

So an email can never go out by an accidental click. Even then, it only goes to your own inbox.

What leaves this tool, and to where?

Company websites: we only read their public pages. Gemini (AI): gets the saved page text. Serper (web search): gets a search like "[company] data breach". Hunter: gets the company's domain. Resend: gets the draft and delivers it to your own inbox only. Neon (database): keeps the runs; contacts are deleted after 30 days.

How do I change the pain points or email wording?

They live in two files, docs/pain-points.md and docs/email-settings.md. Edit them, change "Status: DRAFT" to "Status: APPROVED" when you're happy, and redeploy. They can't be edited from this page on purpose.
